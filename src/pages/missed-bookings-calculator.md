## About the numbers

The booking rates filled in for you are **illustrative starting points, not industry statistics**. They assume that the faster you reply, the more enquiries turn into bookings, which is what most hire businesses find in practice. If you know your own figures, type them in: you'll get a much better estimate.

A simple way to find yours: count last month's enquiries across Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, email, your website form and phone, then count how many became bookings.

## Ways to reply faster

- **Turn on notifications** for every channel, and use one inbox app such as Meta Business Suite for Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp messages.
- **Save replies** for the questions you get every day: prices, availability, delivery areas and garden size.
- **Share the job** at busy times, so someone always answers within the hour.
- **Make online booking easy**, so people who message late at night can check dates and book without waiting for you.
- **Point people to the right page**, such as the castle they asked about, with a link that lets them book straight away.
