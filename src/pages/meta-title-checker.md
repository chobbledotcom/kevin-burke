## How the checker works

Google doesn't cut titles off at a set number of characters. It cuts them off by width: wide letters like "W" and "M" take more room than "i" or "l". This tool measures your text the way a browser does, using the same font size as Google's desktop results. It treats about 580 pixels as the safe limit for titles and about 960 pixels (roughly 155 characters) for descriptions.

The preview is a close guide, not a guarantee. Google sometimes rewrites titles and descriptions, especially when they don't match the page well.

## Tips for hire businesses

- **Put the service and the place first:** "Bouncy Castle Hire in Stockport" beats "Welcome to our website".
- **Add your business name at the end**, if there's room.
- **Write a different title for every page**, especially your town pages and product pages.
- **Use the description to win the click:** what's included, where you cover, and a call to action like "Check dates and book online".
