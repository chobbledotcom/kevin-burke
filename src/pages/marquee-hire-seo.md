## Win more marquee enquiries from Google

Marquee hire is a high-value, researched purchase. Couples planning a garden wedding, families planning a big birthday and event managers all spend time comparing suppliers before they get in touch. Your website needs to rank, and then show them you can handle their event.

### What marquee hire SEO with me includes

- **A clear site structure** with pages for each marquee type, each event type and each area you work in.
- **Event pages** for weddings, garden parties, festivals, corporate events, charity events and winter events, each answering the questions those customers ask.
- **Practical content** on sizes, guest numbers, flooring, lighting, heating, access and site visits, the details that turn browsers into enquiries.
- **Area and venue pages** so you show up across your region, not only near your yard.
- **Links and press** from wedding, event and local business sites once the foundations are in place.

I've worked with marquee and event suppliers alongside bouncy castle and party hire firms. [See how I work](/how-i-work/).


### An example site structure

I start every project by planning the site's structure, then build each page around it. Every business is different, but a typical plan looks something like this:

| Section | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Home page | your marquee types and the regions you cover |
| Marquee pages | frame, traditional, stretch tents and pagodas, with sizes and capacities |
| Event pages | weddings, garden parties, corporate events, festivals |
| Furnishing pages | flooring, lighting, heating, furniture and dance floors |
| Area and venue pages | the counties you cover and venues you have worked at |
| Guides | how big a marquee you need, planning permission, ground conditions |

Once those foundations are in place, I move on to guides, link building and, where it makes sense, press releases. [Read more about how I work](/how-i-work/).

### Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Marquee bookings are planned months ahead. Does that change the SEO?</summary>

Yes. People research marquees early, so guides and planning content matter more. They bring people to your site long before they are ready to book.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Should I have pages for venues I have worked at?</summary>

If you have photos and something useful to say, yes. Venue pages can rank for searches like "[venue] marquee" and show couples what to expect.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can you write about capacities and sizes?</summary>

Yes. Capacity and size information is one of the most searched topics in marquee hire, and clear tables or guides on your site help customers and search engines alike.

</details>
