### An example site structure

I start every project by planning the site's structure, then build each page around it. Every business is different, but a typical plan looks something like this:

| Section | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Home page | your main areas, best-selling castles and how to book |
| Bouncy castles | one page per castle or combi, with size, power and age details |
| Soft play and add-ons | soft play, slides, sumo suits, generators and other extras |
| Area pages | "bouncy castle hire [town]" for each main town you deliver to |
| Occasion pages | children's parties, school fun days, adult castles, weddings |
| Guides | safety, garden space, what to do if it rains, booking tips |

Once those foundations are in place, I move on to guides, link building and, where it makes sense, press releases. [Read more about how I work](/how-i-work/).
