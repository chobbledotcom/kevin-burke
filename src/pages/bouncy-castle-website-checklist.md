## The essentials, before anything else

Before you worry about links or blog posts, make sure your website has the basics right. Most of the bouncy castle sites I see are missing at least a few of these, and fixing them is often the quickest win there is.

Work through the list below and tick off what you already have.

### Your pages

- **A clear home page** that says what you hire out, the main towns you cover and how to book, all near the top.
- **A page for each castle, combi and slide**, rather than one long page listing everything. See my guide to [writing bouncy castle product pages](/bouncy-castle-product-pages-guide/).
- **Pages for your main towns**, each with something useful to say about that area. My [town pages guide](/bouncy-castle-town-pages-guide/) explains how.
- **Occasion pages** for the jobs you want more of, such as children's parties, school fun days, adult castles or weddings.
- **A contact or booking page** that works properly on a phone.

### The details parents look for

- **Prices**, or at least a "from" price. People are far more likely to enquire when they have an idea of cost.
- **Sizes and space needed**, including the access you need through gates and side passages.
- **Power**: whether you supply a generator and how far the castle can be from a socket.
- **Ages and numbers**: who each castle suits and how many can use it at once.
- **Indoor and outdoor use**, and what happens if it rains or is windy.
- **Delivery areas and times**, including any extra charge for further afield.

### Safety and trust

- **Safety testing**: mention your inflatables are PIPA tested and built to BS EN 14960, if they are.
- **Insurance**: say that you hold public liability insurance and how much it covers.
- **Cleaning**: explain how you clean and check equipment between hires.
- **Reviews**: show real reviews on your site and link to your Google reviews. See my [reviews guide](/bouncy-castle-reviews-guide/).
- **Real photos** of your own castles, set up in real gardens and halls.

### Technical basics

- **Works well on phones.** Most parents search and book on their phones.
- **Loads quickly.** Large, uncompressed photos are the usual reason hire sites are slow.
- **Each page has its own title and description**, mentioning the product or town it's about.
- **Your phone number can be tapped to call**, and your business name, address and phone match your [Google Business Profile](/bouncy-castle-google-business-profile-guide/).
- **No broken links or empty pages** left over from old versions of the site.

### Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Do I need online booking?</summary>

It helps, especially for last-minute bookings in summer, but it's not essential. What matters most is that people can see what's available, what it costs and how to get in touch quickly.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Should I put my prices on my website?</summary>

Usually, yes. Parents compare several companies, and many won't enquire if they can't see a price. If prices vary, a "from" price is still helpful.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How many pages does a bouncy castle website need?</summary>

There's no magic number. A small business might have 20 pages, a larger one 100 or more. What matters is that each page has a clear job, whether that's a product, a town or an occasion.

</details>
