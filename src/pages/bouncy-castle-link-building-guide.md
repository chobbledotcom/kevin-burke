## Links that actually help

Links from other websites are one of the ways Google judges whether a business is trusted and relevant. For a bouncy castle company, the best links come from the real world you already work in: schools, venues, local events, community groups and suppliers.

What doesn't help is buying cheap links from unrelated sites. They rarely work for long, and they can do real damage.

### Places to start

- **Schools and PTAs** you've supplied for fun days and fairs. Many list their sponsors and suppliers.
- **Venues** such as village halls, community centres, pubs and sports clubs you deliver to, which may have a list of recommended suppliers.
- **Local events** you support, such as carnivals, fetes and charity days.
- **Other party suppliers** you work alongside, such as entertainers, caterers, cake makers and venues.
- **Local business groups**, chambers of commerce and town websites.
- **Good directories** that are relevant to your area or industry, such as the [directories I run](/directory-network/).

### Give people a reason to link

- **Write up events** you've supplied, with photos, and let the organisers know so they can share them.
- **Sponsor or donate a hire** for a school or charity event, which often earns a mention and a link.
- **Create useful guides**, such as how much garden space a castle needs or ideas for a school fun day, that schools and parents want to share.
- **Share local stories**, such as a new castle, a milestone or a community project, with local news sites.

### What to avoid

- **Paid links from unrelated sites**, link packages and private blog networks.
- **Low-quality directories** that list anyone and everything.
- **Swapping links** with dozens of other businesses just for the sake of it.
- **Spammy comments** on blogs and forums.

### Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>How many links do I need?</summary>

Fewer than you might think. A handful of genuine local links from schools, venues and events can make a real difference for a local hire business.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Are directory links worth having?</summary>

Relevant, well-run directories are worth it, especially ones that focus on your area or on party and event hire. Avoid directories that exist only to sell links.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Should links point to my home page?</summary>

Not always. A link from a school in a particular town is best pointed at your page for that town, and a link about a particular castle at that castle's page. See my [town pages guide](/bouncy-castle-town-pages-guide/).

</details>
