## Hi, I'm Kevin

I'm a Manchester-based web marketer, and most of my clients are bouncy castle, party and event hire businesses. I write their website content, improve their SEO, build links and get them listed on directory sites I run myself.

I started focusing on hire businesses because they have a clear problem I can fix: customers search locally, compare quickly and book whoever looks best on Google. Better pages, better local visibility and a few good links make a real difference to how many enquiries come in.

### Why I keep my prices fair

The online marketing industry is full of companies that charge a lot and do very little beyond analysis. Agencies offering proper content writing or link building often charge a fortune for it. I offer a full SEO and marketing service with high-quality content and relevant, authoritative links at a fair price, as a one-off job or an ongoing service.

### My directory sites

I also run [a few independent directory websites](/directory-network/), each focused on a region or a niche. They list local firms that fit, and clients can appear on the ones that suit their area, which brings extra exposure and referrals.

I work with businesses in all industries. If you need more people finding you online, [get in touch](/contact/).
