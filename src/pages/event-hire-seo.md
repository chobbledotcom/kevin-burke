## More enquiries for your event hire business

Whether you hire out marquees, funfair rides, portable toilets, fencing, sound and screens, photo booths or mobile bars, your customers start the same way: with a Google search. Event organisers, wedding couples, schools and councils compare a few suppliers and enquire with the ones that look right.

I help event hire companies show up in those searches, and look like the obvious choice when they do.

- Funfair, ride and side stall hire
- Luxury and portable toilet hire
- Temporary fencing and crowd control
- Sound, lighting, LED screens and outdoor cinema
- DJs, photo booths, food stations and event extras
- Inflatable games, team building and corporate entertainment

### What I do

- **Service pages** for each thing you hire out, written for the people who book it: wedding couples, event managers, schools and businesses.
- **Location pages** for the areas and venues you work in, so you rank beyond your home town.
- **Event-type content** for weddings, festivals, corporate days, school fairs and community events.
- **Directory profiles** on my network of event and business directories in the UK, Ireland and the USA.
- **Link building, press releases and reputation management** to build trust with Google and with customers.
- **PPC** if you need enquiries quickly for a busy season.

### An example site structure

I start every project by planning the site's structure, then build each page around it. Every business is different, but a typical plan looks something like this:

| Section | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Home page | what you supply, who you supply it to and the regions you cover |
| Service pages | one page per product line, such as marquees, staging, toilets or fencing |
| Event-type pages | weddings, festivals, corporate days, sports events, community fun days |
| Area pages | the counties and cities where you take on events |
| Past events | write-ups of events you have supplied, with photos |
| Guides | planning checklists, site access, licensing and what to ask a supplier |

Once those foundations are in place, I move on to guides, link building and, where it makes sense, press releases. [Read more about how I work](/how-i-work/).
