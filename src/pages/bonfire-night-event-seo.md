## Reach the people organising firework displays

Bonfire Night brings a short, busy run of events. Schools, PTAs, rugby and cricket clubs, Round Tables, Scouts, councils and estates all run firework displays and bonfire parties, usually on the weekend nearest 5 November. Every one of them needs equipment: rides and stalls, toilets, fencing, lighting, PA, generators and catering.

Most displays are planned in late summer and early autumn, once organisers have a date and a venue. That's when your pages need to be ranking.

### Who's searching for Bonfire Night

- **Schools and PTAs** running fundraising firework displays.
- **Sports clubs and charities** that hold an annual bonfire night.
- **Councils and town committees** organising public displays.
- **Estates, farms and venues** putting on ticketed events.
- **Families and neighbours** hosting smaller garden bonfire parties.

### What people search for

- Funfair rides and side stalls for firework displays
- Event toilet hire, fencing and crowd control for bonfire nights
- Lighting towers, generators and PA hire
- Hot food stalls, marshals and event equipment
- How to organise a firework display

### What I build for Bonfire Night

- **A permanent Bonfire Night page** that speaks to organisers, with everything you can supply for a display in one place.
- **Practical detail organisers need**: crowd sizes, toilet numbers, lighting, ground conditions and safety.
- **Past display write-ups** with photos, which show you've done this before.
- **Guides for organisers**, such as planning a school firework display, linking to your Bonfire Night page.
