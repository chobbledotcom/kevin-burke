## Get found by parents planning toddler parties

Soft play hire customers are usually parents of babies and toddlers, planning a first birthday, a christening or a baby shower. They search on their phone, look at a few photos and book whoever looks clean, safe and local. Your website needs to rank for those searches and answer their questions straight away.

### What I do for soft play businesses

- **Site structure and area pages** so you rank in each town and village you deliver to, not just your home town.
- **Package and theme pages** for your soft play sets, ball pits, bouncers, themed packages and add-ons such as balloon arches or mini tables and chairs.
- **Content parents care about:** suitable ages, cleaning routines, how much floor space is needed, hall versus home set-ups and how long the hire lasts.
- **Occasion pages** for first birthdays, christenings, baby showers, nursery events and family parties.
- **Google Business Profile help,** reviews and directory listings to build local trust.

### An example site structure

I start every project by planning the site's structure, then build each page around it. Every business is different, but a typical plan looks something like this:

| Section | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Home page | your soft play packages and the towns you cover |
| Package pages | one page per package or theme, with photos and what is included |
| Add-on pages | ball pits, mini bouncy castles, balloons, sensory play |
| Area pages | "soft play hire [town]" for each main town |
| Occasion pages | first birthdays, christenings, baby showers, nursery events |
| Guides | room sizes, venue tips, cleaning and hygiene |

Once those foundations are in place, I move on to guides, link building and, where it makes sense, press releases. [Read more about how I work](/how-i-work/).
