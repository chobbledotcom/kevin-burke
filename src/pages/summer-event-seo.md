## Fill your summer diary early

Summer is peak season for most hire businesses. School fairs, fun days, festivals, garden parties, sports days, corporate family days and weddings all land in the same few months, and the weekends book up fast.

The people who book the biggest summer jobs plan early. Councils, schools and festival organisers often confirm suppliers in spring, and families start looking for summer party hire as soon as the weather turns. If your summer pages aren't ranking by then, those bookings go elsewhere.

### Who's searching in summer

- **Schools and PTAs** planning summer fairs, sports days and end-of-term parties.
- **Councils and community groups** organising fun days, carnivals and village fetes.
- **Festival and event organisers** looking for rides, toilets, fencing, marquees and power.
- **Businesses** planning corporate family days and summer parties.
- **Families** hiring for garden parties, birthdays and school holiday get-togethers.

### What people search for

- Bouncy castle hire, inflatable slides and water slides
- Fun day hire, school fair hire and village fete attractions
- Funfair ride hire, side stalls and hook-a-duck hire
- Marquee hire for garden parties, weddings and corporate events
- Event toilet hire, fencing and festival equipment

### What I build for summer

- **Event-type pages** for school fairs, fun days, festivals and corporate days, written for the people who organise them.
- **Product pages** for summer favourites such as water slides, assault courses and outdoor games.
- **Area and venue pages**, so you rank for the parks, schools and showgrounds you already work at.
- **Past event write-ups** with photos, which help organisers trust you with bigger jobs.
- **Guides and checklists**, such as planning a school fair or how many toilets an event needs.
- **Directory listings** on the [event and business directories I run](/directory-network/), where they fit.

### When to get ready

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| January to February | Refresh summer pages and add last year's event write-ups |
| March to April | Make sure event-type pages are linked from your home page and area pages |
| May to June | Post availability and offers, and chase reviews |
| September | Write up the summer's events while the photos are fresh |

### Frequently asked questions

<summary>Summer is always busy for me. Do I need summer pages?</summary>

Being busy isn't the same as getting the best bookings. Summer pages help you win the bigger, better-paid jobs, such as council fun days and corporate events, and fill the weekdays as well as the weekends.

</details>
