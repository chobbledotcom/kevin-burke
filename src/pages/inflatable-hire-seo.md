## Rank for "inflatable hire" in every town you cover

Most inflatable hire companies deliver to dozens of towns but only rank in the one they're based in. Customers search "inflatable hire [town]", "bouncy castle hire near me" or "inflatable slide hire" and book from the first few results. I build websites that show up across your whole delivery area, not just your postcode.

### How I approach inflatable hire SEO

- **A proper site structure.** I map out the pages your site needs: a page for each main area you cover, a page for each type of inflatable, and clear links between them, so Google understands what you offer and where.
- **Area pages that read well.** Each page is written for the people in that area: local venues, schools, parks and the occasions people book for. No copy-and-paste templates with the town name swapped.
- **Product and category pages.** Bouncy castles, combis, slides, assault courses, inflatable games, disco domes and adult inflatables, each described with the details customers check before booking.
- **Your existing pages, rebuilt around the structure,** so nothing is wasted and everything links together.
- **Guides, links and press** once the foundations are in place. [See how I work](/how-i-work/).

### An example site structure

I start every project by planning the site's structure, then build each page around it. Every business is different, but a typical plan looks something like this:

| Section | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Home page | your inflatable range and coverage area |
| Category pages | bouncy castles, assault courses, slides, games, adult inflatables |
| Product pages | one page per inflatable, with specs and photos |
| Area pages | "inflatable hire [town]" for each main town |
| Event pages | schools, corporate fun days, festivals, children's parties |
| Guides | safety, power and space requirements, weather advice |

Once those foundations are in place, I move on to guides, link building and, where it makes sense, press releases. [Read more about how I work](/how-i-work/).
