## How to use the numbers

- **Most-used words:** if one word takes up a big share of your page, you may be repeating it too often. Write naturally for the reader and use different ways of saying the same thing.
- **Readability:** the Flesch reading ease score runs from 0 to 100. Above 60 is plain English that most adults read easily. Short sentences and everyday words push it up.
- **Reading time** is based on about 200 words a minute. Speaking time, for videos or voiceovers, uses about 130 words a minute.
- **Character limits:** the page title and meta description figures are approximate, because Google measures them by width. For an exact preview, use the [meta title and description checker](/meta-title-checker/). The Google Business Profile limits are 750 characters for your description and 1,500 for a post.
