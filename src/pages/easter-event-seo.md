## Start your season early with Easter

For many hire businesses, Easter is the first busy spell of the year. The Easter holidays bring family parties, Easter egg hunts, spring fairs and open days at farms and garden centres, and if the weather is kind, the first outdoor bookings of the year.

Easter is also awkward to plan for, because the date moves. It can fall anywhere from late March to late April, so the booking window moves with it. A permanent Easter page that's refreshed every winter means you're ready whichever way the calendar falls.

### Who's searching at Easter

- **Parents** planning parties and get-togethers over the Easter holidays.
- **Churches, schools and community groups** organising Easter egg hunts and spring fairs.
- **Farms, garden centres and attractions** putting on Easter family days.
- **Holiday clubs and nurseries** looking for something special for the break.

### What people search for

- Easter bouncy castle hire and spring-themed inflatables
- Easter soft play hire and party packages
- Easter fun day hire, egg hunt ideas and spring fair attractions
- Funfair rides and side stalls for Easter events
- Things to do at Easter for children

### What I build for Easter

- **A permanent Easter page** for your themed equipment and packages, updated each year with the new dates.
- **Indoor and outdoor options**, because the weather at Easter is never guaranteed.
- **Links from your area pages and product pages**, so local Easter searches find you.
- **Guides parents and organisers search for**, such as planning an Easter egg hunt or an Easter party, linking to your Easter page.
- **Google Business Profile posts** in the weeks before the holidays.

### When to get ready

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| December to January | Check this year's Easter date and refresh your Easter page |
| February | Link it from your home page and post your first Easter updates |
| March to April | Share remaining availability over the holidays |
| After Easter | Add photos from the season and move straight on to summer |

### Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Easter moves every year. How does an evergreen page cope with that?</summary>

The page stays on the same URL every year. Only the dates and availability in the text change, and that takes a few minutes each winter.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is Easter big enough to be worth a page?</summary>

For party and inflatable hire, usually yes. It's a two-week school holiday with plenty of parties and events, and fewer competitors have dedicated Easter pages, so they can rank quickly.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use Easter to get ahead for summer?</summary>

Yes. Easter customers are often the same schools and families who book in summer. Linking your Easter page to your summer pages helps both.

</details>
