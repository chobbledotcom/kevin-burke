### How people in Prestwich search

Most Prestwich searches are short and local. People type "near me", or add Prestwich, Whitefield or Bury to what they're looking for. Google then leans heavily on three things:

- **Your Google Business Profile:** the right categories, accurate opening hours, real photos and regular updates.
- **Reviews:** a steady flow of genuine reviews that mention what you did and where.
- **Your website:** clear pages that say what you offer and which parts of Prestwich and north Manchester you cover.

Get those three right and a small local business can outrank much bigger firms for the searches that matter.
