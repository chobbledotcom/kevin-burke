## Get in front of couples while they're planning

Wedding season usually runs from late spring to early autumn, but wedding bookings don't happen then. They happen months earlier, often a year or more ahead, when newly engaged couples sit down to plan.

Many proposals happen over Christmas, New Year and Valentine's Day, so the weeks after the festive season are when lots of couples start searching for venues and suppliers. If your wedding pages are ranking by January, you're in front of them from the very start.

### Who's searching for wedding hire

- **Engaged couples** planning a marquee, garden or field wedding.
- **Parents of the couple**, who are often the ones researching and paying.
- **Wedding planners** looking for reliable suppliers.
- **Venues** that recommend or bring in marquees, toilets, entertainment and extras.

### What people search for

- Wedding marquee hire, tipi hire and stretch tent hire
- Wedding toilet hire and luxury toilet trailers
- Wedding bouncy castles and children's entertainment
- Photo booths, mobile bars, LED letters and dance floors
- Wedding furniture, lighting and catering equipment

### What I build for weddings

- **Wedding pages for each service**, written for couples rather than event managers, with clear photos and plenty of reassurance.
- **Venue pages** for the venues, estates and farms you supply, which couples search for by name.
- **Real wedding write-ups** with photos, which are some of the most persuasive pages you can have.
- **Planning guides**, such as how many toilets a wedding needs or marquee sizes for guest numbers, linking to your wedding pages.
- **Reviews and testimonials** from couples, shown where people are deciding.
- **Directory listings** on the [event and business directories I run](/directory-network/), where they fit.

### When to get ready

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| October to November | Refresh wedding pages with last season's photos and write-ups |
| December | Make sure everything is live and linked before engagement season |
| January to March | Post updates, chase reviews and add new venue pages |
| September to October | Write up the season's weddings while the photos are fresh |

### Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Weddings are booked so far ahead. When will I see results?</summary>

Wedding SEO is a longer game than party hire. Pages built this winter can bring enquiries for next year's weddings and the year after. The sooner they're live, the sooner they start working.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Should I have separate wedding pages if I already have service pages?</summary>

Usually, yes. Couples search differently from event managers and want different things: reassurance, photos and a sense of style. A wedding version of your key services speaks to them directly.

</details>

Yes. Send me photos (with the couple's permission) and a few notes and I'll turn them into pages that show real weddings at real venues.
