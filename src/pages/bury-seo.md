### Why Bury searches are worth winning

Bury draws people in from well beyond the town, for its market, its shops and the visitors heading to Ramsbottom and the hills. That means two kinds of searcher: locals looking for a service "near me", and people from Rochdale, Bolton, Rossendale and north Manchester searching for a business in Bury by name. A good local SEO plan covers both.

### What I do for Bury businesses

- **Google Business Profile work**, so you show in the map results for searches in and around Bury.
- **Area pages** for the parts of the borough you serve, such as Bury town centre, Radcliffe, Ramsbottom, Tottington, Whitefield and Prestwich, each with genuine local detail.
- **Service pages** that explain what you offer, what it involves and who it's for, written for your customers rather than for search engines.
- **Reviews and reputation**, with a simple way for happy customers to leave a Google review.
- **Local links and listings** from Bury and Greater Manchester sites that make sense for your business.
- **Technical clean-up**, from page speed to missing titles and pages Google can't find.
