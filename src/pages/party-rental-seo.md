## More bookings for your party rental business

Party rental is a crowded local market. Customers compare a handful of companies on Google, look at the photos and reviews, and enquire with the ones that look most reliable. Good SEO puts you in that shortlist, and good content makes you the one they call.

I help party rental and party hire companies in the UK, Ireland and the USA rank for the searches their customers use, from "party hire near me" to "table and chair hire [town]".

### An example site structure

I start every project by planning the site's structure, then build each page around it. Every business is different, but a typical plan looks something like this:

| Section | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Home page | your range, your areas and the parties you cater for |
| Category pages | inflatables, tables and chairs, tents, photo booths, games, catering kit |
| Package pages | ready-made bundles for birthdays, weddings and garden parties |
| Area pages | the towns and cities you deliver to |
| Occasion pages | christenings, proms, Eid, Diwali, Christmas and New Year |
| Guides | party planning checklists, how much space you need, what to hire |

Once those foundations are in place, I move on to guides, link building and, where it makes sense, press releases. [Read more about how I work](/how-i-work/).
