## Be the first call for New Year's Eve

New Year's Eve is one night, but it's one of the most valuable of the year. Private parties, venue events, marquee celebrations and public countdowns all want to book at once, and every supplier is in demand on the same date. Prices are higher, competition for equipment is fierce, and organisers book early to be sure.

That makes NYE well worth a dedicated page. The people searching are ready to book and usually want several things together.

### Who's searching for New Year's Eve

- **Families and friends** hosting house parties and garden marquee parties.
- **Pubs, clubs, hotels and venues** planning ticketed New Year's Eve events.
- **Councils and towns** organising public countdowns and fireworks.
- **Event organisers** running NYE parties, balls and gala dinners.

### What people search for

- New Year's Eve marquee hire and heated marquee hire
- New Year's Eve party hire, DJs, photo booths and mobile bars
- Party furniture, lighting, dance floors and LED letters
- Event toilet hire and luxury toilet hire for winter events
- Heaters, generators and fencing for outdoor celebrations

### What I build for New Year's Eve

- **A permanent New Year's Eve page** that brings your party and event equipment together for the night.
- **Winter detail people need**: heating, lighting, flooring, power and late-night collection.
- **Package ideas**, so customers can see what a complete NYE set-up looks like and book it in one go.
- **Links from your Christmas pages**, because many people plan both at the same time.
- **Google Business Profile posts** from October onwards.
