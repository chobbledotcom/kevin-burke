## Find out what's holding your bookings back

Send me your website and I'll take a proper look, then email you a short list of the changes I'd make first, in plain English. It's free, there's no obligation and I won't pester you afterwards.

### What I check

- **Your Google Business Profile:** categories, service areas, photos, reviews and whether your listing matches your website.
- **Your page titles and descriptions:** whether they mention what you hire out and where, and whether Google will cut them off.
- **Town and area coverage:** whether you have pages for the places you actually deliver to.
- **Your product and category pages:** whether they answer the questions parents, schools and event organisers ask before they book.
- **Technical basics:** broken pages, slow pages, mobile problems and whether Google can find everything.
- **How you compare:** what the businesses above you in Google are doing that you aren't.
