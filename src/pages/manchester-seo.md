## SEO from someone who lives and works in Manchester

I'm based in Prestwich, a few miles north of the city centre, and I help businesses across Greater Manchester get found on Google. Most of my clients hire out bouncy castles, marquees and other event kit, but I also work with trades, shops and service firms closer to home.

When you work with me, you deal with me. I plan the pages, write the content, build the links and report back, so there's no account manager passing messages along and no junior staff learning on your budget.

### What I can do for a Manchester business

- **Local SEO**: a [Google Business Profile](/local-seo/) that's complete and active, consistent business details across the web, and a steady flow of genuine reviews.
- **Area pages**: pages for the boroughs and towns you actually serve, written about those places rather than copied with the name swapped.
- **Content writing**: service pages, product pages, FAQs and guides that answer what your customers ask before they pick up the phone.
- **Link building**: relevant links from local and industry sites that make sense for your business.
- **Technical checks**: fixing the things that hold a site back, such as slow pages, broken links, missing titles and pages Google can't find.

### Covering the whole of Greater Manchester

Customers search by place, so the towns you cover matter as much as what you do. A typical plan might include pages for:

| Area | Places customers search for |
|---|---|
| North | Prestwich, Whitefield, Bury, Radcliffe, Rochdale, Heywood |
| West | Bolton, Wigan, Leigh, Salford, Eccles, Worsley |
| South | Stockport, Sale, Altrincham, Didsbury, Chorlton, Stretford |
| East | Oldham, Ashton-under-Lyne, Stalybridge, Hyde, Denton |
| City | Manchester city centre, Ancoats, Northern Quarter, Salford Quays |

I don't build pages for places you don't serve. Pages for areas you can't deliver to bring the wrong enquiries and can look like spam to Google.
